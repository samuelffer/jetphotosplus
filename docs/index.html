JetPhotos+

A lightweight community extension that adds useful tools directly where you need them — without changing the JetPhotos experience you already know.

JetPhotos+ is an independent, unofficial browser extension. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to JetPhotos.net.

Frequently asked questions

Some important information about the project, its maintenance and how the extension works.

Is JetPhotos+ an official JetPhotos product?+

No. JetPhotos+ is an independent, community-created project and has no affiliation with, sponsorship from, or endorsement by JetPhotos.net.

Is it safe to install?+

The project source code is open and publicly available on GitHub, allowing anyone to inspect and audit it before installing.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?+

Você pode abrir uma sugestão ou relatar um problema na aba Issues do repositório no GitHub.

Does the extension collect my data?+

JetPhotos+ does not send your personal data to its own or third-party servers. Preferences and history needed for some features are stored locally in your browser, and the extension accesses JetPhotos pages only when necessary to provide its features.

Who is behind JetPhotos+?+

JetPhotos+ is developed and maintained by Samuel Ferreira. The project is supported by the Amigos do JetPhotos community, who contribute ideas and help test the extension.
